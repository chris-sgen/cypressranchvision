## Pictures and video made for this redesign

5 stills and 1 loop (one more still was made for the loop, as its first and last frame, and is not shown on its own), made in the early hours of October 26, 2026, Central Time (October 26, between 07:31 and 07:41 UTC). None shows a person, the practice's office, a clinical result, lettering, a label, a logo or a named product. Every still was opened whole and then in enlarged regions at its full size, looking for lettering, numbers, marks that read as a brand, a recognisable product, a person or a hand, a malformed object and a border; the loop was looked at as a sheet of ten of its frames and at its first and middle frames side by side. Each still was made in two candidates, and the better one used; one used still carried a pretend size print along a temple, which was taken off. The files here are re-encodings made by the private build (WebP through a browser canvas, H.264 without an audio track); each carries the AI label (README, Generated imagery) and was read for metadata before publishing (README, Verification). The manifest of the fal.ai originals says that an imperceptible SynthID watermark was applied (a watermark in the picture itself, not in its metadata); this copy did not test for it. 2 remarks of the review:
