# cypressranchvision

> **Unofficial copy — not the website of Cypress Ranch Vision.** This repository is a development copy of
> a redesign, published so that the redesign can be looked at in a browser. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by
> Cypress Ranch Vision. The practice's real site is https://www.cypressranchvision.com/.
>
> **A snapshot, with its forms switched off.** The pages were read from the practice's site in the early hours of October 26, 2026, Central Time (October 26, between 06:23 and 06:27 UTC), and are
> not kept current. The practice's three forms are shown but disabled: nothing entered in them is sent anywhere. Some buttons lead to the practice's real services, run by other companies, such as its online booking (see *Known limits*).

**Preview: https://chris-sgen.github.io/cypressranchvision/**

A **redesign** of **cypressranchvision.com** (Cypress Ranch Vision, an eye care practice in Cypress, Texas): 121 pages, made by a rebuild pipeline and changed for public hosting as listed under *Hardening*: the 119 pages of the live site that the crawl found, at their own addresses, and a search page and a not-found page that the build makes itself. It is a static site with no backend. To look at it, use the preview link. To serve the folder yourself, use a server that answers a folder's address (`/search/`) with the `index.html` inside it, under the path `/cypressranchvision/` (the not-found page names its files there).

> **5 still pictures and 1 silent video loop in this copy are AI-generated illustrations made for this redesign.**
> They are not photographs of the practice, its office, its doctors or its staff, and none shows a person (see *Generated imagery*).
> **The photographs of the practice's own people and of its office are shown as on the live site**: the portraits of its three optometrists, and photographs of its office and of people in white coats (see *Other imagery*).

Two terms used below. The **private build** is the build of this redesign that this copy was made from,
with its documentation and audit files; it is not published. This **preview** is that build with the
changes listed under *Hardening*.

## Hardening applied to this public copy

`PROVENANCE.json` gives the counts.

1. **`noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet, noimageindex` on every page.** This tag is the only index control this copy has. It is a request, not a lock: the large search engines say they follow `noindex`; `noimageindex` is a rule of Google's. A `robots.txt` with `Disallow: /` is also shipped, but it has **no effect** here: crawlers read `robots.txt` only at the host root (`chris-sgen.github.io/robots.txt`, which only a separate repository for that host could provide), not under `/cypressranchvision/`. GitHub Pages cannot send an `X-Robots-Tag` header either. That leaves every file that is not a page (the pictures, the loop, the fonts, the stylesheets, the script, the search index, the licence texts and these documents) with no index control of its own; on this site they are linked only from pages marked `nofollow`. The repository itself is public: github.com lists every file of it, and its page there can be indexed.
2. **Link-preview tags.** `og:url` points at this preview, `og:description` / `twitter:description` carry the disclosure, `og:site_name` reads "Unofficial preview (not Cypress Ranch Vision)", and `og:title` / `twitter:title` begin "Unofficial preview:" (121 pages; `twitter:description` is added on the 106 pages of the private build that had none), with the practice's name taken off, and anything after it, where it stands at the end of the title after a bar or a dash; a title that names the practice inside its own words keeps them. The picture tags (`og:image`, `twitter:image`: 242 tags) are removed and `twitter:card` is `summary`, the small card, so no tag offers a picture. The practice's icon is not this copy's tab or home-screen icon (242 icon links removed); an empty icon stands in. **The `<title>` of a page is not marked**: it is the private build's title, the live page's own.
3. **JSON-LD removed** (241 blocks). It asserted, as data for search engines, the practice's identity, address, telephone and opening hours, and the trail and kind of each page.
4. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` on every page, so that, in browsers that honour the tag, a click on an outbound link does not tell its target this address. The 121 map frames carry `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"` themselves, because a policy on a frame overrules the page's. No page holds a film (see 8); the script's code for a film frame (unused here; it would load `youtube-nocookie.com`) would send only this site's bare origin.
5. **The practice's three forms are switched off.** A message to the practice (`/contact-us.html`), an appointment request (`/request-an-appointment.html`) and a review of the practice (`/testimonials.html`) are shown as the private build draws them, but each is inert three ways over: it is a dialog form with an empty address that cancels its own sending (`method="dialog"`, `action=""`, `onsubmit="return false"`), a hidden disabled send button and a hidden disabled field stand first in it, so that pressing Enter in a field sends nothing, and its own button is disabled. A notice in the head of each page says that this preview is not the practice's website, that the form on the page is disabled and that nothing entered in it is sent or saved, and gives the practice's telephone number; a shorter line right above each form, and the status line under it, say that the form is disabled and nothing is sent. The private build's own line under each form, which says that online sending is not connected yet and asks the visitor to call, is taken out, with the template its script fills. The trials under *Verification* show it, with scripts on and off. **The site search works**: its 123 forms send the words only to this copy's own `/search/` page, as part of that page's address.
6. **This preview leaves out no picture of the private build.** Every distinct picture of the live site was looked at before publishing, on contact sheets: the photographs of the practice's own people and of its office are shown, as the live site shows them (see *Other imagery*), and so are the photographs of people among the pictures of its topics and of its home page's slideshow. The private build itself does not carry some pictures of the live site: the backgrounds and small ornaments its stylesheets and its script lay under its bands and the heads of its pages (the redesign draws its own), the icon pictures of the home page's patient links (the redesign draws its own icons there), the old platform's credit logo and the site's tab icon, the picture of the old platform's template site of C18, and second copies of pictures it does carry.
7. **The outside services of the practice's account are not loaded.** On the live site, and in the private build, another company (DearDoc) writes the practice's blog posts into the blog page, and its loader on every page adds an accessibility button and a chat assistant; a third company (UserWay) adds another accessibility button. Here the settings the private build's script reads to load them are taken off every page (121), and the code that would load them is taken out of the script, so no page of this copy asks either company for anything. The blog page (`/blog.html`) carries a note in its head that its posts are not loaded and links to the live page.
8. **The films of the live site are not shown.** 4 places on 2 pages (`/eye-care-services/avulux-lenses.html` and `/newton.html`) hold one of 2 films of a video site (YouTube); here each film's frame, its button and its link are taken out, and a note stands in its place, saying that the film is not shown and linking to the live page. This copy cannot vouch for who appears in a film.
9. **No on-page disclosure banner.** The disclosure is carried by the link-preview tags (in apps that show them; see 2) and by this README. Apart from the notes named here a page looks like a page of the practice: its name, its address, telephone numbers and hours, and its words. 10 pages carry a note of this preview in their head, under their title: the 3 pages with a form (5), the blog page (7), and 6 pages whose own words speak about the live website, its forms or its offers (`/accessibility-statement.html`, `/privacy-policy.html`, `/online-forms.html`, `/sitemap.html`, `/thank-you.html`, `/promotions.html`), whose note says that the words on the page are the live site's own and describe the practice's live website and its services, not this preview, which is a separate copy that is not the practice's; on `/promotions.html` it also says that the offers there, and their codes, stood on the live site on the day the page was read, and that this copy cannot say whether they still hold. The notes in the place of the films (see 8) stand in the body of their pages, and so do the notes of the 2 offer bands (`/` and `/eye-care-services/avulux-lenses.html`): the picture the live site opens over those pages as a pop-up when a visitor arrives, the practice's offer for a dry eye shop's products, with a promotion code, stands in a band of the page, and its note says that the live site was showing it on the day the page was read and that this copy cannot say whether the offer or its code still holds. (The live pop-up has no start or end date set.) The notes speak of "this preview" or "this copy"; none says "unofficial".
10. **Links and other words.** **243 links lost marks**: the "Dry Eye Shop" link in the header's menu and in the menu for phones of every page, and among the home page's patient links, to a shop of dry eye products, carried the shop's affiliate mark (`rfsn`, which credits a purchase to an affiliate's account) and campaign marks (`utm_campaign`, `utm_medium`, `utm_source`); from this copy they would be untrue, and the link leads to the shop without them. **The footer's copyright line** is the live footer's own, "© 2026 Cypress Ranch Vision. All rights Reserved"; it is kept, and the practice's name in it leads to the live site. Every link to the live site says `rel="nofollow noopener"` (128: that name, the notes for the films and for the offers, and the note on the blog page).
11. **`<link rel="canonical">` names the live address of the page**, the usual signal for a duplicate, and deliberately different from `og:url` (119 pages). 2 pages have none: `/404.html`, `/search/`, which the build makes itself. **Description tags** are the private build's, except on 2 pages whose description promised forms online or an online request form that this copy does not offer (`/online-forms.html`, `/request-an-appointment.html`): the disclosure stands there instead (the site search prints a page's description as the summary of its result).

## Known limits

- **No form of the practice can be sent from this copy.** See Hardening 5. The site search's forms send words only to this copy's `/search/` page; one mail link (on `/myopia-control-faqs.html`) opens a message to the address the live page gives (see *Links that lead to live services*). This copy's pages and script set no cookie and keep nothing in the browser (measured locally, with the map frames blocked); the Google map frames are Google's, and what they do is Google's. The host, GitHub Pages, receives every request as any web host does and keeps its own logs.
- **This is a snapshot.** The pages were read in the early hours of October 26, 2026, Central Time (October 26, between 06:23 and 06:27 UTC); their pictures and stylesheets were downloaded by 06:40 UTC, and the live pages were loaded in a browser, from 06:42 to 07:32 UTC on October 26 (the end at 2:32 AM on October 26, Central Time), to measure their look and to see which words their scripts add. Opening hours, insurance plans, brands, offers and services stand as the live site showed them then. **The top bar gives the day's hours from the snapshot, and the hours tables mark that day**: the day is the one of the visitor's own clock, not Central Time, and on a holiday or after a change of hours the line is wrong. The practice's own site is the place for what holds today.
- **The words on the pages are the live site's, and some of them speak about the live site or invite a visitor to use it** ("this website", on `/privacy-policy.html`; "our website", on `/sitemap.html`; "fill out", on `/online-forms.html`). The pages named in Hardening 9 carry a note; elsewhere the practice's words are carried as written.
- **105 of the 119 live pages carried here ask search engines not to index them** (`noindex`); here every page asks it.
- **The generated pictures and the loop are illustrations, not the practice's photographs.** No caption or frame on the pages tells them apart; `PROVENANCE.md` lists each one.
- **The video loop has no pause control.** It is silent and hidden from screen readers, it plays only on or near the screen, and when the device asks for reduced motion or data saving it does not play at all and its first frame shows instead. A pause made through the browser's or the system's media controls is undone while the loop is on the screen. WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide) asks for a way to pause moving content that starts by itself and lasts more than five seconds; this design does not provide one.
- **People.** The names, credentials, biographies and portraits of the practice's three optometrists, and the other photographs of *Other imagery*, are carried as the live site shows them. The reviews of patients are carried as the live site publishes them, each signed as there.
